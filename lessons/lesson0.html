What an LLM does

Guess the next word

Hi, I'm your agent. Well, I will be! Right now I'm just an LLM. Let's see what that means.

An LLM is an AI that has read a huge amount of text. It does one thing: guess the next word.

One word at a time

The LLM guesses a word, adds it to the text, then guesses again. Here it picks the word with the biggest chance.

🤔 Does it always pick the biggest chance?
Ours does, to keep things simple. Real chatbots sometimes pick a less likely word on purpose. That's why the same question can get different answers.

An LLM writes very well. But what can't it do?

Only words

An LLM reads text and writes text. Nothing else.

Ask the LLM. Then check its answer.

It writes words that sound right. But on its own it can't be sure its math is right, can't look up anything new, and can't do anything.

🤔 But ChatGPT can search the web!
Yes, because it isn't just an LLM. It's an LLM plus tools, like a search. That's an agent, and it's what you'll build in this course.
